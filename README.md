>
> Google Play listings that use the **RuelzMusify name, logo, app icon, screenshots, or other official branding** are **unofficial third-party listings**, even if they look identical to this project. They are **not verified or supported by the RuelzMusify maintainers** and should **not be mistaken for official releases**. Their contents and safety cannot be guaranteed.
>
> **Get the official RuelzMusify only from trusted sources:** [GitHub Releases](https://github.com/rukamori/RuelzMusify/releases/latest), [Obtainium (via the official GitHub repository)](https://apps.obtainium.imranr.dev/redirect?r=obtainium://add/https://github.com/rukamori/RuelzMusify/), or Early-access builds are provided through the [official RuelzMusify Discord](https://discord.gg/ruelzmusify).
>
> If you encounter a Google Play listing impersonating RuelzMusify, **report it to Google Play** and [notify the maintainers](https://github.com/rukamori/RuelzMusify/issues/new/choose).

> [!WARNING]
**Forks are not supported in any way.**  
We do not provide support, maintenance, troubleshooting, or assistance for any forked version of this project.
If you choose to use a fork, you do so at your own risk.

**RuelzMusify** isn’t just another generic YouTube Music wrapper. It’s a fully custom-built player made with kotlin and jetpack compose, because we think your music should stay private, look clean, and work exactly the way you expect.

> [!IMPORTANT]

## ✨ Features

<div align="center" id="features">

<table>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>Playback</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Multiple account support with quick switching</li>
          <li>Ad-free playback with background listening</li>
          <li>Sponsor blocks support</li>
          <li>Your playlists, liked songs, and subscriptions appear after sign-in</li>
          <li>Support local file and local song playback</li>
          <li>Customizable player and notification interface</li>
          <li>Highly support android auto</li>
          <li>Fast startup and lightweight performance</li>
          <li>Built for a private, uninterrupted listening experience</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>Audio</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>EBU R128 loudness normalization</li>
          <li>Bit perfect and DAC support</li>
          <li>Tempo, pitch, and playback speed controls</li>
          <li>Crossfade between tracks</li>
          <li>Customizable Equalizer, Equalizer system and spatial audio integration</li>
          <li>Hi-res audio playback modules.</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>Lyrics &amp; Discovery</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Live synced lyrics</li>
          <li>Apple music lyrics feels</li>
          <li>Lyrics translation, AI Lyrics translation and romanization</li>
          <li>Music recognition for songs around you</li>
          <li>Listening statistics whenever you want them</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>Sync &amp; Social</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Import playlist from spotify</li>
          <li>Online and offline backup and restore support</li>
          <li>YouTube Music account integration</li>
          <li>Last.fm, libre.fm and custom scrobbling</li>
          <li>ListenBrainz history sync</li>
          <li>Customizable discord rich presence support</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
  </tr>
  <tr>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">
        <h3>Interface</h3>
        <ul>
          <li>Built with material 3 expressive</li>
          <li>Multi customizable interface</li>
          <li>Album-art powered dynamic colors</li>
          <li>Up to 11 different player styles</li>
          <li>Up to 8 different player background styles</li>
          <li>Responsive layouts for different screen sizes</li>
          <li>Animated Artwork while playing</li>
          <li>Clean browsing, player, artist, album, and lyrics views</li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </td>
    <td width="50%" valign="top">
      <div align="left">

          <li>Animation and layout tuning</li>
